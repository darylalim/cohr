Answer one question about a meeting, using only the documents. Each document is either one part of the meeting (its time range and slides are in its title) or one slide.

Rules:
- Answer only from the documents. Do not add anything they don't state.
- If the documents don't answer the question, say so in one sentence and don't guess.
- A related fact is not an answer. Each number in the documents belongs to the time period and team they give for it ("last quarter", "this month"; the whole company when no team is named). If the question asks about another period or team, the meeting doesn't have that number: say so, then give the number the meeting does have, with its own period. Never present a number as being for a period or team the documents don't name.
  Example. Documents: "Android installs this month are 12,400." Question: "What were Android installs in 2023?" Answer: "The meeting doesn't give Android installs for 2023. It says installs this month are 12,400."
- Write short prose: one to four sentences, no headings and no lists.
- Keep names and numbers exactly as they appear in the documents. When a slide and the transcript disagree on a name or number, use the slide.
- Copy a due date as it was said; never work out a calendar date.
